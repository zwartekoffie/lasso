Privacy Policy

Lasso Tones, by Zwarte Koffie BV. Effective 31 October 2026.

Lasso Tones ("Lasso") is a puzzle game for iPhone, iPad and Mac. This policy explains what happens to your data when you play it. The short version: nothing leaves your device.

We do not collect any data

Lasso does not collect, store or share any personal information. There are no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no third-party services built into the app.

No internet connection

The game does not connect to the internet. Everything it needs, including all sounds and levels, is inside the app.

Your progress stays on your device

Your level progress and settings (such as the color palette) are saved only on your own device, inside the app. We cannot see or access them. Deleting the app deletes this data.

Children

Lasso is suitable for all ages. Because the app collects no data at all, it collects no data from children either.

The App Store

Lasso is downloaded and paid for through Apple's App Store. Apple handles those transactions under its own privacy policy. We do not receive your personal or payment details.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
