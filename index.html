Lasso Tones support

A physics puzzle game you can hear, by Zwarte Koffie BV.

Need help, found a bug, or have an idea? Email timokuilder@gmail.com and mention your device (for example "iPad Air") if something is not working.

How to play

Press on a dot and drag the rope around other dots. Let go back where you started to close the loop. Falling balls bounce off your ropes, and every bounce plays a note on the beat. Guide a ball into the white goal to finish the level.

Grab a rope you drew to change its shape, or unwind it back off every dot to untie it.

I hear no sound

Check that your volume is up, then tap the screen once. If you just came back to the game from another app, the first tap brings the sound back.

A level is locked

Levels unlock one by one. Finish a level to open the next.

Where is my progress saved?

On your device only. Deleting the app also deletes your progress.

Jam

"Jam" on the title screen is free play: every rope, no goal. Draw and listen.
